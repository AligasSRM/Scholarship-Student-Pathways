# Scholarship Student Pathways

A structured system for researching, comparing, preparing, and managing fully funded or near-zero-cost university pathways for international students.

## Core rule: ZERO-OUT-OF-POCKET
A pathway is marked ZERO-COST ELIGIBLE only when official sources explicitly cover the mandatory costs needed to start and continue study.

We track: tuition, compulsory fees, application fees, visa, medical examination, translation/legalisation, language/tests, airfare, accommodation, insurance, food/living, books, local transport, and other mandatory charges.

Never assume coverage. If an official source does not confirm a cost, mark it UNCOVERED / VERIFY.

## Workflow
1. Build student education profile.
2. Verify qualification and equivalency.
3. Research exact university/programme entry requirements.
4. Research exact scholarship coverage.
5. Build official document pack.
6. Identify every mandatory cost and payer.
7. Check deadlines and application sequence.
8. Audit eligibility and documents.
9. Submit and keep confirmation evidence.
10. Track admission, scholarship, visa, travel, arrival, and enrolment.

## Evidence rule
Prefer official university, government, embassy, scholarship, immigration, and testing-provider sources.

Do not guess certificate names, grades, document numbers, equivalency, eligibility, coverage, or deadlines.

## Privacy
This repository is PUBLIC. Never upload passports, IDs, birth certificates, addresses, phone numbers, private emails, signatures, medical records, or scans of official documents. Use anonymised student IDs.
