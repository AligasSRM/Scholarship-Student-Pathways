# Romania — MFA Scholarship

For the 2026/27 call, Bachelor's applicants needed high-school/pre-university completion with a baccalaureate diploma or equivalent and recognised study documents.

Bachelor and Master scholarship programmes are taught in Romanian; a preparatory Romanian-language year is available where applicable.

Coverage includes tuition, accommodation in student dormitories within the allocated subsidy, and monthly financial aid.

Important: the official FAQ states applicants bear costs inherent to a visa application. Therefore this route is not automatically zero-out-of-pocket.

Official guidance also requires passport, birth certificate, academic diploma/certificate, transcript, medical certificate, proof of permanent address/ID and applicable language evidence. Academic documents require certified translation and applicable legalisation/apostille.
