# Türkiye — Türkiye Scholarships

Official general criteria state minimum academic achievement for associate/bachelor candidates is 70% and the general age criterion is under 21.

Full-time coverage includes university/programme placement, monthly stipend, tuition, one-year Turkish language course, accommodation, health insurance, and one-off flight tickets at the beginning and end of study.

Age is a hard eligibility gate for the general undergraduate route.

Türkiye Scholarships also has associate-degree scholarship programmes for technical and vocational education and training. Exact eligibility and available programmes must be checked against the student's qualification.
