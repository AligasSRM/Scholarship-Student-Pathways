# Brunei — Government of Brunei Darussalam Scholarship

## Official coverage
Brunei MFA scholarship terms state coverage including tuition and listed compulsory fees, economy-class airfare to Brunei and return after completion, BND 500 monthly personal allowance, BND 150 monthly meals allowance, BND 600 annual book allowance, residential accommodation, baggage allowance on completion, and comprehensive insurance.

The application requires certified academic/supporting documents, certified English translations for non-English documents, certified birth certificate and passport copies, and references/recommendations.

## University admission
The scholarship is separate from university admission. UBD and UTB applicants also need the relevant university application.

UBD states that undergraduate applicants must meet general and programme-specific requirements and that recognised equivalent qualifications may be considered.

## Critical check for our student
A Syrian Commercial Vocational Secondary Certificate in Accounting must NOT be assumed equivalent to a direct bachelor's entry qualification. Obtain official confirmation from the exact university.

## Cost audit
Visa, medical, translation/legalisation and other pre-departure costs must be checked against the current intake terms before declaring ZERO-COST.
