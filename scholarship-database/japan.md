# Japan — MEXT Undergraduate

Official Study in Japan guidance states undergraduate applicants must be under 25 and have completed 12 years of school education or an equivalent course comparable to Japanese high school.

MEXT provides a monthly allowance, exemption of entrance examination/matriculation/tuition fees, and an economy-class airline ticket under stated conditions.

The official guideline also identifies travel to the nearest international airport, airport taxes/fees, some transit-country travel/lodging, and travel insurance in specified circumstances as costs the student may bear.

Therefore this route is NOT automatically zero-out-of-pocket.

The student's Syrian vocational qualification must be evaluated against the 12-year/equivalent requirement.
