# China — Chinese Government Scholarship

## Current undergraduate requirement
Official Chinese guidance for 2026/27 states undergraduate applicants must take the China Scholastic Competency Assessment (CSCA) before applying and provide a valid CSCA score report. Required subjects depend on the target university and major.

## Required university-by-university checks
- Exact undergraduate programme
- Teaching language
- Acceptance of Syrian vocational secondary qualification
- Equivalency
- CSCA subjects
- Language requirement
- Scholarship category
- Tuition
- Accommodation
- Medical insurance
- Stipend
- Airfare
- Application channel
- Application fee
- Deadline
- Nomination requirements

Do not mark ZERO-COST until the exact university and scholarship category confirm all mandatory costs.

Official starting point: https://www.campuschina.org/
